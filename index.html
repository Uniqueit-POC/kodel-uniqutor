Leading the Future of Premium Leather

A journey of craftsmanship, from India to the world.

Since our foundation in 2016, Kodel has leveraged 30 years of industry expertise to become a premier manufacturer of high-quality, animal-free leather solutions. Committed to sustainable and ethical manufacturing, we serve the automotive, fashion, and furniture sectors across 10+ countries, delivering over 500,000 sq.ft. of premium material monthly from our state-of-the-art facilities to our flagship showroom in Thailand.
